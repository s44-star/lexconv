A LexConv initiative / 02

Mahakal Lab.
Better systems for medicine access.

Exploring practical infrastructure for medicine access, distribution coordination and greater visibility across healthcare supply chains.

Our purpose ↓
01 / OUR PURPOSE

Help the existing
system work better.

Access to essential medicines depends on reliable information, legitimate supply and coordination between the people and organisations already serving patients.

Mahakal Lab explores ways to support those processes with practical workflows and, where appropriate, software or hardware. The aim is to complement legitimate healthcare providers and distributors—not replace them.

Medicine supply and delivery must follow applicable local laws, licensing rules, prescription requirements and professional oversight. This page describes the initiative's focus, not a claim of active medical distribution or regulatory approval.
02 / AREAS OF WORK
01 / ACCESS

Medicine access coordination

Explore practical ways to communicate medicine needs to appropriate, authorised healthcare and supply partners.

02 / DISTRIBUTION

Distribution workflows

Study order coordination, hand-offs and delivery processes that support reliable, accountable distribution.

03 / VISIBILITY

Supply-chain visibility

Explore information workflows that can improve visibility into availability, order status and distribution bottlenecks.

04 / INTEGRITY

Product integrity

Consider practical approaches to traceability and verification, working within the capabilities of authorised partners.

05 / TECHNOLOGY

Software and hardware

Evaluate lightweight tools for coordination and monitoring where they address a demonstrated operational need.

06 / COLLABORATION

Support existing providers

Prioritise cooperation with legitimate pharmacies, clinics, distributors and other authorised healthcare actors.
