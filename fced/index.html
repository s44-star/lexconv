Research and practical initiatives to expand productive employment, strengthen enterprise development and improve access to economic opportunity.

Our purpose ↓
01 / OUR PURPOSE

Economic opportunity
must reach further.

FCED focuses on the practical conditions that help people access productive work and enterprises become sources of sustainable employment.

Our approach connects economic research with employer demand, enterprise needs and potential delivery partners. The aim is to identify workable pathways from opportunity to real economic participation.

FCED is an initiative under LexConv. This website describes its focus and intended areas of work; it does not imply government affiliation, verified employer partnerships or completed job-placement outcomes.
02 / AREAS OF WORK
01 / EMPLOYMENT

Productive employment

Explore employer demand, paid-work opportunities and practical pathways to connect people with legitimate work.

02 / ENTERPRISE

Enterprise development

Study the constraints faced by small and emerging enterprises and identify opportunities to strengthen their ability to create jobs.

03 / RESEARCH

Economic research

Examine employment barriers, market needs, workforce participation and the conditions that support local economic resilience.

04 / TECHNOLOGY

Modern work systems

Assess how digital tools, outsourcing and remote-work models can support access to legitimate income opportunities.

05 / INCLUSION

Wider participation

Give particular attention to underserved communities and barriers that limit women's access to paid work.

06 / COLLABORATION

Practical partnerships

Seek constructive collaboration with employers, enterprises, researchers and relevant institutions where there is a clear shared purpose.
