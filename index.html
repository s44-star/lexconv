The problem

Talent exists.
Markets don't always connect.

Many smaller and frontier economies have capable entrepreneurs, viable businesses, and willing workers. What's often missing isn't ability — it's connection: to distribution, to capital, to digital infrastructure, to international customers, and to the kind of business development that turns a viable idea into a growing company.

The FEC model

How growth becomes employment

We don't manufacture jobs.
We build the businesses that can create them.

Employment is strongest when it's attached to real economic demand. FEC therefore begins with businesses, customers, and markets — not with a predetermined number of jobs.

About

Built by LexConv.

LexConv is the company behind FEC. The broader mission is to build practical businesses that solve real problems — FEC is the international growth model that grew out of that work.

LexConv is led by Subham Praharaj, based in Odisha, India, with family roots in farming and cooperative societies — the same logic behind FEC's approach to growth: build the structure a business can grow into, rather than waiting for growth to happen on its own.

Outside LexConv, Subham also runs the Padmini Foundation, supporting young female athletes in East Africa, beginning in Burundi.
